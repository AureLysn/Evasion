## 1. Firefly's three deaths

Source of the "three deaths": a "script" (剧本) given to her by 艾利欧 (Elio; the vault calls him "Destiny's Slave"). She says it aloud in 2.2: 「…我会在梦想之地经历三次「死亡」。」 and 「第一次是如同死亡般的痛苦，我的身体被「沉眠」的翼刃贯穿，才有了后来所有的故事。」 She also says she will carry out the second, and that the unfinished third should not come "以最糟糕的样子". (上升的一切必将汇合, 2.2) https://wiki.biligame.com/sr/上升的一切必将汇合 (high)

**Who or what attacked her the first time:** the Memory Zone Meme 忆域迷因「何物朝向死亡」, shortened in dialogue to 「死亡」. After the 2.2 events it is renamed 「沉眠」: 姬子 says 「你和流萤小姐就是在这里遭遇了「死亡」——现在该叫它「沉眠」了。」 (等醒来再哭泣) https://wiki.biligame.com/sr/等醒来再哭泣 (high). Bestiary entry 忆域迷因「何物朝向死亡」: https://wiki.biligame.com/sr/敌人一览 (high).

**Death 1, as seen in 2.0 (mission 倘若在午夜醒来):**
- BWIKI mission summary: 「你在梦境酒店大堂发现流萤。「死亡」袭来，将流萤抛向高空，用翼刃贯穿少女的身体。流萤落入你的怀中，彻底化成碎裂的梦泡。」 Her last words are 「对…不起……」. 黄泉 (Acheron) apologises that, had she drawn her blade then, she might have saved Firefly. Mission description: 「流萤消失在了「死亡」的翼刃下。」
- Present: 三月七, 姬子, 瓦尔特, 黑天鹅, 银狼, 黄泉, 流萤.
- https://wiki.biligame.com/sr/倘若在午夜醒来 (high)

**Death 1, as retold in 3.8 (mission 分成两半的迷梦):**
- Elio told her she would die three times in Penacony. She calls this one the "first death" and wants to choose how it comes: 「艾利欧曾说，我会在匹诺康尼经受三次死亡。而这「第一次死亡」…我想自己决定它如何到来。」
- She goes to the Meme before 黄泉 can draw her blade (her "零点任务": keep the Trailblazer away from Acheron's blade). The Meme pierces her and she turns into a pool of light-blue liquid. She then arrives at 流梦礁 (Dreamflux Reef) and later returns as 萨姆 (Sam) and fights 黄泉.
- The page footnote says this cutscene is the same one as in the 2.0 mission 倘若在午夜醒来.
- https://wiki.biligame.com/sr/分成两半的迷梦 (high)

**Death 2:**
- 2.2: she says she now understands the second death and will carry it out (see the 2.2 source above). 黄泉 says she woke early through "一次真正的「死亡」", because as a stowaway she had no dream-entry device and no help from the Family. She then found the Express and brought the news of the Order remnants. (然后，在第八天… https://wiki.biligame.com/sr/然后，在第八天… , high)
- 3.8: in 世间的每一个清晨 she fights 「示死祸源：深魇蝗灾」, wins and takes the Stellaron, tears an opening in the 原始梦境, and "迎来了自己的第二次死亡" by falling into 太一之梦. 银狼: if the Trailblazer beats 星期日, her death becomes only a dream. If not, the dream merges with reality and her death becomes real. https://wiki.biligame.com/sr/世间的每一个清晨 (high)

**Death 3 (shown only in 3.8):**
- In 终将实现的 she re-counts. Being sent to 流梦礁 was not a death, so "真正的三次死亡" are: (1) she cannot dream, so once she wakes she can never re-enter and her dream self will cease to exist; (2) 寰宇蝗灾 kills her and she revives with her real power; (3) that power makes her entropy-loss syndrome (失熵症) accelerate until she dies of it. Quotes: 「第二次，是寰宇蝗灾杀死了我，我启用了自己真正的力量，死而复生。」 「但那力量，必然将导致第三次——我的症状会不可抑制地加快，失熵而死。」
- https://wiki.biligame.com/sr/终将实现的 (high)
- In 2.x the third death is only foretold, never shown (上升的一切必将汇合, line 519).

**How the 3.8 ending differs from 2.x:**
- 2.x ending: Firefly dies (death 1), then "wakes" via a real death, brings the Order-remnant information to the Express (然后，在第八天…), and lives in reality with 银狼, wanted by the 猎犬家系 (没有被征服的 https://wiki.biligame.com/sr/没有被征服的 , high). No fading from entropy loss is shown.
- 3.8 终将实现的 (mission description): 「由于之前的经历，她的失熵症在你面前恶化，彻底消失。」 「借助「终末」的力量，你让过去发生了一些小小的偏折，补全了流萤的遗憾。」
  - Scene: on the 晖长石号 viewing spot she tells the Trailblazer to close their eyes. Her sight and mind dissolve; 「然而流萤的目光再也没有移动过」; then 「眼前的少女消散了…」 as the Trailblazer uses Finality to turn back time and grant her wish (a white-day-dream with fireworks).
  - Epilogue (Express chat, high): 银狼: 「她醒了」, 「暂时没事了」, 「卡芙卡带她回来的时候也这样」, 「老是昏迷 机甲也开不了」, 「不过治得好」. So the dream-Firefly fades, and the real Firefly survives and recovers.
- Summary: 2.x = she leaves through a "true death" and survives; 3.8 = third death (entropy loss) shown in dream as fade-out, undone or softened by Finality; she survives in reality.

Also: BWIKI mission summary of 树上的灾兆 says 花火 asked her 「那如果…你会在美梦中，迎来三次死亡呢？」 https://wiki.biligame.com/sr/树上的灾兆 (high). In 异乡异客 (2.3) a Sparkle "text doll" says someone paid Sparkle to be the "director" and make the "三次死亡" happen as safely as possible (see section 3).

## 2. Dahlia

- zh: **大丽花**, real name **康士坦丝** (Constance); English The Dahlia. Path 虚无 (Nihility), element Fire. Faction in the infobox: **焚化工**. Release 2025-12-17 (3.8). https://wiki.biligame.com/sr/大丽花 (high)
- Character story, 角色故事•一 and 二: she fled from 奈落 and burned all memories of herself; she is a child of **冥火大公** (Duke Inferno) from 永火官邸, the eldest-like "sister" of the other children ("书吏", "枷锁", "调音师"), who walks the Memokeepers' halls as an 忆者 (high).
- 角色故事•三 and the line in the 3.8 mission text: she is described as a "collaborator" of the Stellaron Hunters. A Hunter's twisted memory: 「我拜托她烧掉无名客与「终末」相关的记忆」 (she burns Nameless memories on request). Whether that Hunter is Firefly is not stated (see Not found).
- 角色故事•四: in the script where she joins the Hunters, she rises as 「须臾」, a 毁灭 "大君" of 记忆.
- 3.8 behaviour (all high, BWIKI mission pages):
  - 另一个，同一个: she comes alone to the Express, guides the Trailblazer through restoring Penacony memories and tells the truth of 终末. She first appears disguised as 黑天鹅 and weaves false scenes (大丽花扮作黑天鹅). https://wiki.biligame.com/sr/另一个，同一个
  - 分成两半的迷梦: she says she was also in the Trailblazer's Penacony memories, catches Firefly in the 原始梦境, and calls herself 焚化工. 黑天鹅 says she was pursued by the 反物质军团; she says the 流光忆庭 hunted her fourteen times, and the last time she died before Black Swan's eyes. 黑天鹅 calls her 「狱友」. 黑天鹅 also says the Duke has no child named 康士坦丝. https://wiki.biligame.com/sr/分成两半的迷梦
  - 我弥留之际: 黑天鹅 exposes her as the Dream Lord's (梦主歌斐木) collaborator, not a Hunter, who posed as a companion of several factions using 忆者 powers. She teleports Firefly away and burns Robin's memory of the meeting. She and 黑天鹅 stage a fight so that 黑天鹅 burns the 「律令」 the Dream Lord shared with her. At 「橡木鸣蛀之梦」 she reveals the Dream Lord turned himself into four 「律令」 to recreate the 寰宇蝗灾 in the dream. https://wiki.biligame.com/sr/我弥留之际
  - 终将实现的: she absorbs and burns all the 「无」 (Nihility) the dream took on after 黄泉 drew her blade, and is badly hurt. After the farewell she helps the Trailblazer recover burned memories. 大丽花: 「醒来吧，这场梦该结束了。」 https://wiki.biligame.com/sr/终将实现的
  - Relation to Firefly: stated in the missions as an ally at first (she catches Firefly, stays with her in 分成两半的迷梦, shows her a memory of AR-214), then exposed as the Dream Lord's collaborator and sends her away. 大丽花 later says Firefly hid and went away as the "third death" came (终将实现的: 「她知道「第三次死亡」会是多么悲惨…」). No kinship or family tie stated.
