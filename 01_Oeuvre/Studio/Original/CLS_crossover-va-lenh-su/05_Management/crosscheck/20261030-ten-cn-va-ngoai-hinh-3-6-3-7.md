How a term was matched: an English term counts as matched to a Chinese term only when both sit in the same mission step or dialogue line. The two wikis transcribe the same line of the game in two languages.

Confidence key:
- **high**: the Chinese was seen in game text on BWIKI.
- **medium**: the Chinese was seen only in Fandom's zh-CN field.
- **low**: indirect.

## Appearance

These describe only what is visible in the official art hosted on Fandom. Colours are as rendered.

- **Evernight (长夜月)**. Sources: splash art https://honkai-star-rail.fandom.com/wiki/File:Character_Evernight_Splash_Art.png and full-body portrait https://honkai-star-rail.fandom.com/wiki/File:Character_Evernight_Portrait.png
  - Hair: very long, pale silver-white at the crown, shading into pink and lilac toward the long trailing lengths.
  - Eyes: red.
  - Headpiece: small black tiara or crown-like ornament on the head.
  - Clothing: short black long-sleeved coat-dress with a high collar, white puffed undersleeves at the wrists, a dark red lining or accent and a red gem at the waist, and a silver chain hanging from the front. Under it, white ruffled layered skirt tiers that trail behind.
  - Gloves: black.
  - Legs and shoes: bare legs, with a dark garter or strap on one thigh. Ruffled white ankle socks and black heeled ankle boots with red heels and soles.
  - Earring: a red, tassel-like dangling earring.
  - Props in the splash: a heart-shaped vanity mirror on a wooden desk, melting white candles, red petals, a photograph on the floor, translucent white ribbons, and a large pale lilac butterfly or moth shape.
  - In-game line, referent unconfirmed: in 大地啊，承载万物的苦难, Bartholos (巴特鲁斯) complains about 「那个粉毛红眼睛的疯女人」 ("that pink-haired, red-eyed madwoman") who was watching him. The context implies Evernight, but the line does not name her.
- **Anaxa (那刻夏)**. Source: splash art https://honkai-star-rail.fandom.com/wiki/File:Character_Anaxa_Splash_Art.png
  - Hair: short silver-grey, with longer strands at the back.
  - Eyes: the visible eye is red/pink; the other is hidden by the pose and hair.
  - Clothing: a dark navy/black short capelet-jacket with gold trim over a teal/blue high-collared garment, and a long blue-teal coat tail or skirt panel with gold buttons. A red cuff accent shows on one upper arm.
  - Gloves: black.
  - Belt: a belt with a red gem and a hanging chain.
  - Legs and shoes: dark trousers and blue/teal shoes with red soles.
  - Props: holding a blue flame. Background has a green-glowing broken stained-glass window, a classical column, roots and bookshelves, and small pale spirit figures.
- **Herta in the Amphoreus arc**: NOT FOUND. No source describing a different model was found.
  - In BWIKI 3.6/3.7 dialogue the speaker label is mostly 黑塔. It appears as 大黑塔 twice, in 英雄啊，归以凡身向侵晨 (the battle against Irontomb).
  - Fandom's The Herta page (大黑塔) has no appearance section.
- **Dan Feng (丹枫)**. Source: official NPC image https://honkai-star-rail.fandom.com/wiki/File:NPC_Dan_Feng.png. No official text description was found.
  - Hair: black, long, with lighter brownish ends visible at the crown.
  - Horns: one pale teal/blue-white horn is visible at the side of the head.
  - Ears: pointed.
  - Earring: a tassel earring with a red tassel.
  - Clothing: a white wide-sleeved outer robe with grey/navy cloud-like shoulder pieces and white crane or feather motifs, over grey layered inner robes. A teal gem clasp sits at the chest.
  - Gloves: black.
  - Eyes: closed in this image, so eye colour is not shown.
