> *However, Operation Zulo came at a heavy cost to the Galaxy Rangers. Facing imminent defeat despite the remnants of Swarm aiding them, La Mancha unleashed the shadow of Voracity sealed within his right arm as a last-ditch effort to secure victory. The shadow subsequently consumed Zulo, the remnants of Swarm, and many Galaxy Rangers alike.[3] In the aftermath, La Mancha incurred the ire of "Heartless Bear" and "One-Eyed Owl," who accused him of dragging the Rangers into ruin for the sake of a personal vendetta. Following the incident, La Mancha quietly disappeared. While seeking La Mancha's whereabouts, "Owl" tracked down the brothers on Moria Ecological Island that La Mancha was providing for and gouged out their eyes, hoping to see La Mancha's location through them.[1]*
